Welcome to the second part of my data journey. In this folder you will find my study materials as I prepare to tackle the Microsoft Azure PL-300 exam. I am starting here because I want to widen my scope, get into data analysis first before data engineering, and this certification is my launchpad into this exciting new field.

The goal is to gain knowledge and experience in data analysis concepts and see how they come to life in Microsoft PowerBI.
I am using this space to document my notes and key takeaways so that I can track my progression and look back on how far I have come.

To prepare for this exam, I am following a structured study plan using a mix of great community resources. 
<br> 
My main source is [Phillip Burton' PL-300 course](https://www.udemy.com/course/70-778-analyzing-and-visualizing-data-with-power-bi/) on Udemy. I will also test my knowledge with the official practice tests from the Microsoft website alongside other mock exams, custom NotebookLMs and capping everything off with [Pragmatic Works's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BDGzVpt1xCg) to serve as cram.


The focus of my study covers the four main pillars of the exam:
* Prepare the data (25–30%)
* Model the data (25–30%)
* Visualize and analyze the data (25–30%)
* Manage and secure Power BI (15–20%)
